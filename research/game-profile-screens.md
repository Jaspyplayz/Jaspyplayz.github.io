# Game profile screen layouts

## Question

How do the League of Legends, Valorant and Marvel Rivals profile and career screens arrange a player's identity, rank, stats and match history? Which of those patterns carry over to a one-page portfolio styled as a game profile screen for Jasper Li (UofT CS; "match history" = Turnpoint, Petri, X Arena; peak ranks = Grandmaster in League, Eternity in Marvel Rivals; plus skills and contact links)?

## Findings

### League of Legends

- **Overview tab:** a full-bleed champion splash fills the background (the most-played champion by default, and players can change it). A vertical **banner on the left** stacks the player name, an optional title, challenge tokens, the summoner icon with its border, and the level. A season flair at the end of the banner shows last season's rank. Ranked armor, mastery score and the most-mastered champion run along the **bottom strip**. [wiki.leagueoflegends.com/Summoner_profile](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner_profile) (secondary)
- **Tabs** across the top: Overview, Challenges, Match History, Ranked, Highlights. Detailed stats live inside each match-history row, not on a stats page of their own. (same source, secondary)
- **Identity order, from Riot:** icon in the centre, then the Challenge Crystal, then the name with the **title "directly beneath your summoner name"**, then up to three tokens. Hovering a token shows its tier. [Challenges Walkthrough](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/challenges-walkthrough/)
- Riot's identity roadmap describes ranked emblems as a "wing-shaped" piece that can be reused elsewhere, and says titles will reflect skill or lore. Banner Skins are meant to let players "more prominently feature your past rank." [Progression Identity Changes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/progression-identity-changes/); [Challenges Walkthrough](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/challenges-walkthrough/)

### Valorant

- Identity comes in three formats: a tall **Card** (lobby, Collection), a wide **Banner** (loading screen, and the killer's profile an enemy sees) and a small **Avatar** (friends list, leaderboard). [valorant.fandom.com/Player_Card](https://valorant.fandom.com/wiki/Player_Card) (secondary)
- The **Career** page tracks Act Rank progress and past acts. The Act Rank Badge is a **triangle of up to nine wins**, where "wins at higher ranks visually replace your lowest wins." Its border upgrades at 9, 25, 50, 75 and 100 wins. Friends can open your match history and see your rank there. [Act II Ranks and Competitive Changes](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/act-ii-ranks-and-competitive-changes/)
- Career Match History is a list of **match rows** with filter buttons and a dropdown on the left. Patch 12.06 adjusted the text sizes inside each row. [Patch Notes 12.06](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-12-06/)

### Marvel Rivals

- The **Career** screen has an Overview page (it supports picking specific hero forms), a rank **leaderboard display**, and a "Career - Customize" area for choosing nameplates and Championship Emblems. [Patch 20250411](https://www.marvelrivals.com/gameupdate/20250401/41548_1223996.html)
- Other players can open your career profile, which shows per-hero **proficiency** scores. [Patch 20250307](https://www.marvelrivals.com/gameupdate/20250304/41548_1215931.html)
- The ranks above Grandmaster are **Celestial, Eternity and One Above All**. Crests of Honor reward Grandmaster and above, so a peak rank becomes a cosmetic you can display. [Dev Talk Vol.10](https://www.marvelrivals.com/devdiaries/20250210/40954_1210988.html)

## Patterns that translate to a one-page portfolio

- **Hero section = League banner and splash:** a big background image, with a vertical banner on the left holding the avatar with a border, "Jasper Li", and the title "UofT Computer Science" directly under the name. Up to three "tokens" for headline skills.
- **Peak-rank flair:** two rank emblems side by side ("Peak: Grandmaster, League" and "Peak: Eternity, Marvel Rivals"), shown as wing or crest badges in the spirit of League's last-season flair and Rivals' Crests of Honor. Label them "peak" so they read honestly.
- **Match history = project rows:** one Valorant-style row each for Turnpoint, Petri and X Arena. Each row has an icon, name, role or stack, a short "result" line and links, and expands for details the way League puts stats inside each match entry.
- **Skills = proficiency:** Rivals-style proficiency bars or levels per skill, used in place of a generic tag cloud.
- **Tab bar as anchor nav:** Overview / Match History / Skills / Contact as sticky in-page tabs, which keeps it one page.
- **Card / Banner / Avatar reuse:** one identity element in three sizes, for the hero, the sticky header and the favicon or Open Graph image.
- **Contact as "Add friend":** links (GitHub, LinkedIn, email) styled as social buttons on the banner.
